## Trigger workflows with webhooks

Webhooks send submission data to your endpoint as soon as a response is received. Connect OpnForm to custom backends, automation platforms, internal dashboards, and more.

## Setup guide

1. Navigate to **OpnForm > Integrations** in your form editor.
2. Select **Webhook** and enter your endpoint URL.
3. Map fields and configure triggers.
4. Save and activate the webhook.

## When to use it

Use webhooks when you need direct integration with an API, custom automation, data warehouse, CRM, or another internal tool.
