## Save responses to Google Sheets

The Google Sheets integration keeps submission data organized in a spreadsheet. Use it for reporting, lightweight databases, operations workflows, or shared team review.

## Setup guide

1. Navigate to **OpnForm > Integrations** in your form editor.
2. Select **Google Sheets** and connect your Google account.
3. Choose or create the target spreadsheet.
4. Save and test the integration.

## When to use it

Use Google Sheets when your team wants a simple, collaborative place to review form responses and build lightweight workflows around them.
