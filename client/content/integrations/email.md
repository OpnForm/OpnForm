## Get form submissions in your inbox

Email notifications help your team react quickly when a form receives a new response. Send alerts to one recipient or multiple teammates so every submission reaches the right people.

## Setup guide

1. Navigate to **OpnForm > Integrations** in your form editor.
2. Select **Email** from the integration list.
3. Configure recipients and notification rules.
4. Save and activate email alerts.

## When to use it

Use email notifications for contact forms, lead capture, support requests, applications, or any workflow where a teammate needs to see new submissions immediately.
