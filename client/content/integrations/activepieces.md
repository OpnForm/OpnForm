## Build automations with Activepieces

Activepieces lets you connect OpnForm submissions to automated workflows without code. Route form responses into apps, notifications, CRMs, spreadsheets, and custom processes.

## Setup guide

1. Navigate to **OpnForm > Integrations** in your form editor.
2. Select **Activepieces** or create an Activepieces flow with a webhook trigger.
3. Map form fields to the apps and actions in your flow.
4. Test the flow and turn it on.

## When to use it

Use Activepieces when you want open-source no-code automation for lead routing, alerts, spreadsheet updates, CRM actions, or internal operations.
