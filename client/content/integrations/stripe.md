## Accept payments with Stripe

Stripe lets you collect payments directly from your forms. Add paid registrations, deposits, orders, donations, or invoices to your submission workflow with secure checkout.

## Setup guide

1. Navigate to **OpnForm > Integrations** in your form editor.
2. Select **Stripe** and connect your Stripe account.
3. Configure pricing, currency, and payment labels.
4. Publish the form and test checkout.

## When to use it

Use Stripe when your form needs to collect payments, registration fees, deposits, or paid requests at the time of submission.
