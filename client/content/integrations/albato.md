## Connect workflows with Albato

Albato lets you connect OpnForm submissions to the apps and services your team already uses. Automate follow-ups, data sync, notifications, and internal processes from every response.

## Setup guide

1. Open Albato and create a new automation for OpnForm submissions.
2. Connect OpnForm through the available app or webhook trigger.
3. Map submission fields to the actions in your automation.
4. Test and activate the automation.

## When to use it

Use Albato when you want no-code app automation for lead management, data syncing, notifications, and operational workflows.
