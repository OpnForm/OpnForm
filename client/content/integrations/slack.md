## Send form alerts to Slack

Slack notifications keep form activity visible in your team workspace. Route submissions into the right channel so sales, support, operations, or recruiting teams can act fast.

## Setup guide

1. Navigate to **OpnForm > Integrations** in your form editor.
2. Select **Slack** and authorize your workspace.
3. Choose a channel and customize the message.
4. Save and activate Slack alerts.

## When to use it

Use Slack notifications when submissions need quick team visibility, such as inbound leads, incident reports, customer requests, or internal approvals.
