## Send submissions to Discord

Discord notifications bring new form responses into the servers where your community or team already collaborates. Keep moderators, event teams, or support channels informed in real time.

## Setup guide

1. Navigate to **OpnForm > Integrations** in your form editor.
2. Select **Discord** and connect your server.
3. Choose the channel that should receive alerts.
4. Save and activate Discord notifications.

## When to use it

Use Discord alerts for community forms, event signups, bug reports, or any workflow managed from a Discord server.
