## Send alerts to Telegram

Telegram notifications deliver OpnForm submissions to the chats where your team stays connected. It is a simple way to keep response activity visible from mobile or desktop.

## Setup guide

1. Navigate to **OpnForm > Integrations** in your form editor.
2. Select **Telegram** and connect your bot or chat.
3. Choose where notifications should be sent.
4. Save and activate Telegram alerts.

## When to use it

Use Telegram for mobile-first teams, operations alerts, community forms, and fast response workflows.
