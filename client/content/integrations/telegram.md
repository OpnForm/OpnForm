## Receive submission alerts in Telegram

Connect a Telegram account to OpnForm and receive messages when your form gets a new response. Keep important requests visible in a chat tool you already check, especially when your work takes you away from a desktop inbox.

Use notification content that summarizes the request and helps you decide whether to respond now or review the complete submission later. The integration delivers an alert; it does not replace your customer communication or request tracking process.

## Telegram integration use cases

### Stay aware of service inquiries
Get a concise alert when a potential customer requests a quote or describes a problem. Include the request type and preferred contact details so you can plan follow-up between appointments.

### Monitor event registrations
Keep an organizer informed when new registrations arrive. Use the form to collect structured details, and Telegram to flag new activity without repeatedly checking the submissions dashboard.

### Watch an internal request form
Receive notifications for equipment needs, maintenance reports, or scheduling requests. The message gives the responsible person a prompt to review the request in context.

## Set up Telegram notifications

1. Open your form's **Integrations** tab and add **Telegram Notification**.
2. Use **Connect Telegram account**, or select a Telegram account already available in your connections.
3. Configure the available notification message options and save the integration.
4. Submit a sample response and check that the connected Telegram destination receives the alert.

## Send the context you need

Keep notifications short enough to scan on mobile. Avoid copying sensitive free-text responses into a chat unless that destination is appropriate for the information. Make sure someone owns the next step after receiving the alert.

## Common questions

### Can I change the connected account?

Use your connection settings to connect another Telegram account, then select it in the form's integration settings.

### Can Telegram also create a CRM record?

The native integration sends submission messages. For additional actions in other apps, use an automation platform such as [Activepieces](/integrations/activepieces).

For team channel notifications, explore [Slack](/integrations/slack) or [Discord](/integrations/discord).
