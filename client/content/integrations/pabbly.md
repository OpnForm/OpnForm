## Automate with Pabbly Connect

Pabbly Connect helps you move OpnForm submissions into business workflows. Trigger actions in CRMs, email tools, spreadsheets, support desks, and other apps when someone submits a form.

## Setup guide

1. Open Pabbly Connect and create a workflow for OpnForm submissions.
2. Connect OpnForm using the available integration or webhook trigger.
3. Map form fields to your workflow actions.
4. Test the workflow and enable it.

## When to use it

Use Pabbly Connect when you want no-code automation for sales, marketing, operations, and support workflows powered by form submissions.
