## Automate with Zapier

Zapier helps you connect OpnForm submissions to the tools your team already uses. Build no-code automations for CRMs, spreadsheets, email tools, project management systems, and more.

## Setup guide

1. Open Zapier and choose OpnForm as the trigger app.
2. Connect your OpnForm account.
3. Choose the destination app and map submission fields.
4. Test and turn on your Zap.

## When to use it

Use Zapier when you want to connect OpnForm with multiple apps without building or maintaining custom integrations.
